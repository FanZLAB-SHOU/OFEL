PhD Students

Mingfeng Jiang

PhD Student, Year 3

jmf822221@163.comGitHub (opens in a new tab)ResearchGate (opens in a new tab)

Mingfeng Jiang is a PhD student in Fisheries Resources at Shanghai Ocean University, supervised by Professor Fan Zhang. After studying civil engineering as an undergraduate, he moved into marine fisheries science for his master’s and doctoral studies.

His research focuses on cephalopod population dynamics and stock assessment, particularly jumbo squid in the southeast Pacific. He develops models that account for death after spawning and examines how ENSO shapes squid migration and reproduction. He also evaluates seasonal fishing closures as a means of conserving oceanic squid. He received a 2026 National Scholarship.
